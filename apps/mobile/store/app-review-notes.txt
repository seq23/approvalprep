1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached in App Review Information (ApprovalPrep-App-Review.mp4). iOS Simulator, from launch: the free dispute letter questions, the finished letter, saving it as a PDF, and the Letter of Explanation Kit up to Apple's purchase sheet (the Simulator cannot complete sandbox purchases). Every kit and the bundle are one-time In-App Purchases submitted with this version; in the sandbox, tap a kit on Home, then "Get this kit" ("Restore purchase" is on the same screen). The app has no account registration, login or user-generated content, so there is no account deletion, reporting or blocking flow.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
ApprovalPrep helps US adults write their own letters to credit bureaus and creditors, such as disputing a credit report error or a letter of explanation for a mortgage, rental or loan application. Many people pay a credit repair company to send letters they may send themselves for free (FTC, "Fixing Your Credit FAQs"). The app asks one plain question at a time and turns the person's facts into a first draft to save as a PDF, print or share.

3. HOW TO USE IT (no login needed)
- Open the app. There is no account and no sign-in.
- Home: tap "Free credit bureau dispute letter" (free, unlimited). Answer the questions (bureau, the account and what is wrong). The letter appears at the end. Tap "Save or share PDF" or "Print".
- On the letter, "Mark as sent" keeps the mailing date (free). The day count and a day-30 reminder on this phone come with a kit (nothing is sent to us). My letters lists saved letters.
- Kits (listed on Home): one-time In-App Purchases, no subscription. The Credit Letter Kit and Letter of Explanation Kit add more letter types inside the app, with answer-date tracking; every kit also gives its PDF guide and editable Word file to save or share. "Restore purchase" is on every kit screen, and "Restore a purchase" is in More.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Apple In-App Purchase, managed through RevenueCat (purchases and restore).
- approvalprep.com (hosted on Cloudflare): serves each purchased kit's PDF and Word files after the purchase is verified, plus the help, privacy, terms and disclaimer pages.
- No AI services, analytics SDKs or third-party sign-in. Letter answers stay on the device and are never uploaded.

5. REGIONS
Written for US credit bureaus and US law (FCRA). Features and content are the same in every region.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY
ApprovalPrep is not a credit repair organization. It does not contact bureaus or creditors, sends nothing on the user's behalf, and promises no removal or score change. It sells self-help letter templates, and the free dispute letter needs no purchase. Every letter screen says: "Self-help templates. Not legal or financial advice. We do not contact bureaus or creditors for you." Disclaimer: https://approvalprep.com/credit-repair-disclaimer/

7. KITS ALREADY BOUGHT ON OUR WEBSITE (Guideline 3.1.3(b), Multiplatform Services)
The same kits are also sold on approvalprep.com. A person who already bought a kit there can open that same kit in the app at no extra charge: More > "Bought on approvalprep.com?", paste the order link from their download page or confirmation email. Only the order code is sent; our server confirms the paid order and the app unlocks that kit.
- Every kit that can be opened this way, and the bundle, is also sold in the app as a one-time In-App Purchase submitted with this version, with the same content.
- Home and every kit screen offer only the In-App Purchases. No website prices, checkout links or pointers to buy outside the app; the website-order option is only in More, for people who already own a kit.
- To see it without a website order: More > "Bought on approvalprep.com?", paste any text and tap "Open my website kit". The app replies that it does not look like an ApprovalPrep order link and nothing unlocks.
