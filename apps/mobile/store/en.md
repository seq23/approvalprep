# ApprovalPrep: Credit Disputes (store description draft)

Subtitle: Skip the credit repair company. ApprovalPrep is not a credit repair company: you write and send the letters yourself.

ApprovalPrep helps you write your own credit dispute letter, letter of explanation or rental application letter.

Who it is for: anyone with an error on a credit report, and anyone a lender, landlord or employer has asked to explain something on a mortgage, loan, apartment or job application.

What it costs: the credit bureau dispute letter is free, as many times as you need. Every kit is a one-time purchase. No subscription and no monthly fee.

ApprovalPrep is not a credit repair company: you write and send every letter yourself. The app walks you through one question at a time and turns your true facts into a clear first draft. Save it as a PDF, print it or share it.

- Free: the Letter Studio, a quick first draft for any of 9 situations.
- Eight products, each paid once, no subscription: Letter of
  Explanation Kit, Income + Employment Letter Kit, Credit Letter Kit, Rental Application Kit,
  Loan Prep Letter Kit, Business Funding Prep Kit, Life Admin Letter Kit and the Complete
  ApprovalPrep Bundle. Each kit opens the website kit's PDF guide and editable Word file.
- The Credit Letter Kit and the Letter of Explanation Kit also work inside the app: goodwill, debt
  validation, settlement terms and follow-up letters, and nine explanation letters. Mark each
  letter as sent and My letters keeps the date, so you know where you stand.
- Already own a kit? The bundle upgrade means you never pay for it twice.
- No account and no upload. Your answers stay on this phone.

Accurate information usually stays on a report. A letter is a request, not a
promise. You can dispute errors with the credit bureaus yourself for free; this
app helps you write the letter.

Self-help templates. Not legal or financial advice. We do not contact bureaus or creditors for you.
