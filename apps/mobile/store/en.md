# ApprovalPrep: Dispute Letters (store description draft)

Subtitle: DIY credit repair alternative. ApprovalPrep is not a credit repair company: you write and send the letters yourself.

Write the letters a credit repair company would send, and send them yourself.

ApprovalPrep walks you through one question at a time and turns your true facts
into a clear first draft. Save it as a PDF, print it or share it.

- Free: the credit bureau dispute letter, as many times as you need.
- The same eight products as approvalprep.com, each paid once, no subscription: Letter of
  Explanation Kit, Income + Employment Letter Kit, Credit Letter Kit, Rental Application Kit,
  Loan Prep Letter Kit, Business Funding Prep Kit, Life Admin Letter Kit and the Complete
  ApprovalPrep Bundle. Each kit opens the website kit's PDF guide and editable Word file.
- The Credit Letter Kit and the Letter of Explanation Kit also work inside the app: goodwill, debt
  validation, settlement terms and follow-up letters, and nine explanation letters. Mark each
  letter as sent and My letters keeps the date, so you know where you stand.
- Already own a kit? The bundle upgrade means you never pay for it twice.
- No account and no upload. Your answers stay on this phone.

Accurate information usually stays on a report. A letter is a request, not a
promise. You can dispute errors with the credit bureaus yourself for free; this
app helps you write the letter.

Self-help templates. Not legal or financial advice. We do not contact bureaus or creditors for you.
