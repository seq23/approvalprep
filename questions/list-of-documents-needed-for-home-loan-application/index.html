List of Documents Needed for Home Loan Application

A list of documents needed for a home loan application is the complete set of financial paperwork required by lenders to verify your income, employment, assets, debts, and identity before approving a mortgage.

Published 3 November 2026 · ApprovalPrep

What income documents do lenders require for a home loan?

Lenders need to verify that you have a steady and reliable income to make your monthly mortgage payments. You must provide your most recent pay stubs covering at least thirty days. You also need your W-2 forms from your employers for the past two years. If you receive bonuses, overtime, or commission income, lenders will examine your history of receiving these funds.

For self-employed borrowers, independent contractors, or small business owners, the documentation requirements are more detailed. Lenders typically ask for complete federal tax returns for the past two years, including all schedules and business returns if you own a corporation or partnership. A year-to-date profit and loss statement and a balance sheet signed by you may also be requested to show current business earnings. Always ensure your tax returns are signed and include all pages before submitting them to your lender.

How do you document your assets and funds for closing?

You must prove you have enough money for your down payment, closing costs, and cash reserves after purchase. Lenders require complete bank statements for all checking and savings accounts for the past two to three months. These statements must show every page, even if some pages are blank. Do not redact any information, as underwriters need to see account numbers and institutional names clearly.

If you have retirement accounts, investment portfolios, stocks, bonds, or mutual funds, provide the most recent account statements. Any large or unusual deposits appearing in your bank accounts will require a written explanation and a paper trail, such as a gift letter or sale receipt. Lenders examine these funds closely to ensure you are not taking on undisclosed debt for your down payment.

What proof of employment and identity do you need to provide?

Lenders must verify your employment status and history before granting a mortgage. You will need to provide the contact information for your current employer, including the name, address, and human resources or supervisor phone number. Underwriters will often contact your employer directly during the underwriting process to confirm you are still actively employed.

You also need to prove your legal identity and residency status. Lenders require a government-issued photo identification, such as a driver license or passport. Permanent resident aliens must provide a green card, while non-permanent residents need valid visa documentation. You may also be asked for your Social Security number to allow the lender to pull your credit report and verify your credit history.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to provide tax returns if I am a W-2 employee?

Most W-2 employees only need to provide W-2 forms for the past two years along with recent pay stubs. Lenders only request tax returns if you have additional income sources like rental properties or self-employment.

What counts as a large deposit on a bank statement?

Lenders typically view any deposit that exceeds fifty percent of your total monthly household income as a large deposit. You must provide a documented paper trail proving the source of these funds.

How many months of bank statements do lenders ask for?

Lenders generally require the most recent two to three consecutive months of statements for all checking, savings, and investment accounts. All pages of each statement must be included in your submission.

Can I use gift funds for my home loan down payment?

Yes, many loan programs allow gift funds from family members. You must provide a signed gift letter stating the money is a gift with no expectation of repayment, along with proof of transfer.
