# Press Brief — 2026 ApprovalPrep Document Readiness Index

## Headline
ApprovalPrep catalogs 100 public application-checklist sources across rental, employment verification, mortgages, and small-business funding.

## What is new
- 100 direct public source URLs, balanced at 25 per category.
- A downloadable CSV with source type, format, access date, and review depth.
- Item-level findings restricted to eight primary sources marked deep reviewed.
- A correction policy and limitations section designed for citation safety.

## Usable findings
- Application guidance is split between HTML pages and PDFs.
- Instructions are distributed across government agencies, financial institutions, universities, housing organizations, and private operators.
- Deep-reviewed primary sources repeatedly center factual identity, income/employment, assets/fund sources, and traceable supporting records.

## Citation boundary
Do not describe the dataset as a survey of applicants, approval outcomes, or market share. Do not claim that every source asks for every document. Cite the report URL and, when discussing a particular requirement, cite the underlying primary source.
